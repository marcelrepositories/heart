Make a mess. Then put it away.

Thirty-six tiles, one home each. Drag them anywhere. Press Tidy and watch every one go back where it belongs.

Or press S, then T.

Tap a tile to knock it loose. Tap it again to send it home. Hold one to drag it.

01

Three rules

01

Every tile has one home.

Tile 01 lives top left, tile 36 bottom right, and the rest read like a page. A loose tile stays exactly where it was left until something sends it home. Moving a mess around is not the same as tidying it.

02

Nothing gets lost.

Scatter as hard as you like. A tile never leaves the board, and Tidy always finds all thirty-six. Afterwards even their stacking order is sorted, which nobody will notice, which is the point.

03

Every mess is repeatable.

Scatter is seeded, not random. Your Mess #4 is everyone's Mess #4, and the address bar always says which one you are looking at. Copy the link and send somebody your mess. They can tidy it for you.

02

Keep it tidy

Best in this browser · none yet

Press Play and the board starts knocking tiles loose, one at a time, each a little sooner than the last. Send each one home before the next goes: tap it, drag it, or reach it with the arrow keys and press Space. The run ends when nothing is in place, or when you press Tidy, which puts everything away and stops the clock. Runs are numbered like messes, so your Run #7 is everyone's Run #7; the board does not play favourites.
